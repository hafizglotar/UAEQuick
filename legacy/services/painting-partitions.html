FAQ - Painting Services in Dubai

How much does painting cost in Dubai?
Room painting starts from AED 350, a 1-bedroom apartment from AED 1,500, a 2-bedroom from AED 2,500 and a full villa from AED 5,000. Prices include labour and standard emulsion paint.
Do you supply the paint or do I?
Either works. We supply Jotun, National Paints or Dulux as standard, or we paint with what you provide and reduce the quote accordingly.
How long does a paint job take?
Single room 4-6 hours. 1-bed apartment 1 day. 2-bed apartment 2 days. Villa 3-5 days depending on size and prep.
Do you do gypsum partitions and false ceilings?
Yes. Gypsum partitions from AED 350 per square metre, false ceilings from AED 120 per square metre, including frame, board and finishing.
Do you cover furniture and floors?
Yes. All furniture covered, floors protected with drop cloths. We sand and wash walls before painting as standard.
