Dubai Interior Design - From Concept to Handover

UAE Quick delivers full interior design and fit-out services across Dubai. We take projects from first concept sketch through to handover: site survey, mood boards, 3D renders, technical drawings, approvals, joinery, electrical, plumbing, painting, flooring and final styling.

Our Al Quoz workshop produces custom kitchens, built-in wardrobes, TV units and bespoke furniture in-house, which means tighter quality control and faster lead times than outsourced studios. We handle apartments, townhouses, villas, offices, restaurants and retail spaces.

All projects include Dubai Municipality and building-management approvals where required. Book a free site visit and we'll bring sample materials and concept sketches to your space.

FAQ - Interior Design in Dubai

How much does interior design cost in Dubai?
Interior design in Dubai typically costs AED 80-250 per square foot depending on scope. A 1-bed apartment full fit-out from AED 70,000, a villa from AED 250,000. Fixed scope-of-work quote after free consultation.
Do you handle approvals and permits?
Yes. Dubai Municipality, building management and Civil Defence approvals for fit-out work where required.
Do you supply furniture and joinery?
Yes. Custom joinery, built-in wardrobes, TV units, kitchens and bespoke furniture from our Al Quoz workshop.
How long does a typical project take?
1-bed apartment full fit-out 4-6 weeks. Villa 8-14 weeks. Office or restaurant 6-10 weeks.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes. Initial site visit and concept consultation are free. Detailed design and 3D renders are quoted separately.
