Why Dubai Marina Apartments Need Specialist Service

District cooling (Empower & Tabreed) is different from split AC

Most Marina towers don't have outdoor compressor units — they use chilled water from a district cooling plant (Empower or Tabreed) piped through fan coil units (FCUs) in each apartment. This means: no gas to refill, no outdoor unit to clean, but FCU filters clog faster, drain pans block more often, and thermostat faults are the most common complaint. Our technicians are trained specifically on Marina FCU systems.

Balcony and window damage from salt and sand

Marina apartments overlooking the sea (JBR-side towers especially) suffer faster wear on balcony glass, aluminium frames and outdoor metalwork. We handle balcony deep cleaning, glass restoration and silicone resealing as part of our cleaning and handyman service.

Move-in/out is high-stakes in Marina

Marina has one of Dubai's highest rental turnovers. Building management deducts heavily from security deposits for poor move-out condition. Our move-out cleaning checklist is built around the specific deductible items Marina tower managers flag most: oven interior, AC filter condition, balcony tile, kitchen grout, and bathroom silicone.

Lift bookings, permits, and after-hours access

Most Marina towers require: lift booking for moves (4-hour slots), contractor passes for painting/electrical work, and after-hours permits for noisy work. We handle all of this — you don't need to call building management yourself.
