What's Different About Service in JLT

Ageing plumbing is the #1 JLT issue

Most JLT towers are 12 to 18 years old. Flexible hoses, bathroom silicone, kitchen mixer tap seals and washing-machine connections are the most common failure points. Our JLT plumbers carry parts for the most common JLT fittings (Grohe, Hansgrohe, Roca, Vitra) so most leak calls are resolved in a single visit.

Empower district cooling - FCU service, not gas refill

JLT runs on Empower chilled water. Apartments have FCU (fan coil units) hidden above false ceilings or in service ducts. Common issues: drain pan overflow (often the cause of "leaks" reported by units below), filter clog from desert dust, and thermostat faults. We carry replacement Empower-compatible thermostats.

Move-out inspection in JLT is strict

JLT building managers run thorough move-out inspections. Top deduction reasons: AC filter condition, oven interior, balcony tile, bathroom silicone, kitchen extractor grease. Our JLT move-out cleaning checklist is built specifically around these inspection items.

Cluster access can vary

Some clusters (especially Cluster D, T and X) have stricter contractor access protocols. We hold standing approval with most major JLT building managements - for any tower requiring fresh approval, we handle the paperwork.
