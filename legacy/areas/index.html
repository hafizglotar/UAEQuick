One Price List Across All of Dubai

Whether you're in a Marina studio or a Palm Jumeirah villa, our apartment-service rates are identical: AC cleaning AED 150 per unit, handyman from AED 80, deep cleaning from AED 450 for a 1-bed, painting from AED 1,500 for a 1-bed. FAQ - Dubai Service Areas

Which Dubai areas does UAE Quick cover?
Every Dubai community. Featured areas with dedicated pages: Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, JLT, Jumeirah, Palm Jumeirah, Arabian Ranches and JVC. We also serve Mirdif, Deira, Bur Dubai, Silicon Oasis, JVT, Motor City, Sports City, Al Barsha, Emirates Hills, Discovery Gardens, International City, The Greens, Meadows, Springs, Bluewaters, City Walk, Al Furjan, Dubai Hills, Damac Hills and DIFC.
What is the average arrival time across Dubai?
Arrival times range from 20 minutes (Business Bay, Al Barsha) to 45 minutes (Arabian Ranches, Palm Jumeirah Frond villas, Silicon Oasis). Most Dubai areas are covered within 25-35 minutes from our Al Quoz base.
Are prices the same across all Dubai areas?
Yes. Apartment service pricing is identical across all Dubai areas - AC cleaning AED 150 per unit, handyman from AED 80, deep cleaning from AED 450. Villa pricing varies by villa size, not by community.
Which Dubai areas need specialist service?
Palm Jumeirah and beachfront Jumeirah villas need salt-corrosion AC service and marine-grade paint. Older JLT towers need plumbing specialists familiar with ageing fittings. Downtown and Marina towers need contractor pre-clearance to avoid building delays.
Do you serve areas outside Dubai?
Our primary coverage is Dubai. We accept inter-emirate jobs for moving (to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman) and large project work (interior design, villa painting). For routine maintenance outside Dubai, we recommend a local provider.
