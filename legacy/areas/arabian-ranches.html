Arabian Ranches' Trusted Villa Services Team

Arabian Ranches is one of Dubai's largest and longest-established gated villa communities — 4,000+ family villas spread across three phases and around 20 sub-communities, anchored by the Arabian Ranches Golf Club. Service priorities here are different from beachfront or apartment districts: established lawns and mature trees need consistent weekly care, family villas often have private swimming pools that need year-round chemical balancing, and most homes have 4–8 split AC units across multiple bedrooms and living areas.

UAE Quick serves over 200 villa contracts across Arabian Ranches — most on combined pool + garden weekly maintenance. We know each sub-community's quirks: Phase 1 (Hattan, Mirador, Saheel) villas are 15+ years old, typically due for repaint and AC overhaul; Phase 2 (Casa, Camelia, Yasmin, Lila) are newer with tighter Emaar finishing; Polo Homes are premium and demand presentation-perfect service.

Available services include weekly pool maintenance, garden and palm care, multi-unit AC servicing, villa painting, villa deep cleaning, handyman and plumbing, electrical work and interior design. Call 058 933 1711 for a free villa site visit.

What's Different About Service in Arabian Ranches

Combined pool + garden contracts are the norm

Most Arabian Ranches families book pool and garden maintenance together because the same weekly visit can cover both — saving the call-out cost and giving you one point of contact. We send a 2-person crew (one pool tech, one gardener) for a 60-90 minute combined visit. Photo report after every visit.

Phase 1 villas are due for systematic refresh

Hattan, Mirador and Saheel villas were delivered in 2007-2009. After 16+ years, most need: full repaint (every 5-7 years), AC overhaul (compressors at end of life), bathroom silicone reseal, kitchen mixer tap replacement, and irrigation system overhaul. We offer a Phase 1 villa refresh package combining these in a single scheduled project.

Phase 2 & 3 villas need preventive care, not repair

Newer Phase 2 and 3 villas (Casa, Camelia, Yasmin, Joy, Spring) are still in warranty period for most components. Our focus here is preventive: weekly pool + garden contracts, twice-yearly AC servicing, annual plumbing inspection. Catching issues early before they become claim disputes with the developer.

Community management approvals — we handle them

Emaar Community Management governs most exterior work in Arabian Ranches. Painting villa exteriors, replacing landscape features, or contractor vehicles parked on community roads all need notice or approval. We file the paperwork for you and obtain approval before scheduling work.
