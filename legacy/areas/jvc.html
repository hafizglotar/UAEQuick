JVC's Trusted Home Services Team

What's Different About Service in JVC

New-build snagging is the #1 JVC service

Developers in JVC hand over hundreds of units per month. Most have minor defects: door alignment, gypsum cracks at corners, paint touch-ups around sockets, AC drain pan misalignment causing slow drips, missing skirting trim, silicone gaps between bathroom fixtures and walls. We provide a written snagging report with photos and recommended fixes — usable for developer claims during the warranty period.

First-occupancy AC commissioning prevents problems

Many JVC apartments sit empty for months before first occupancy. AC drain lines dry out, filters collect construction dust, thermostats remain in default mode. Our first-occupancy AC service flushes drains, replaces filters, runs a full system test and sets your preferred zones — preventing surprise issues in week one.

Rental turnover service for investor units

A high share of JVC apartments are investor-owned and rented out. Between tenants, we offer a turnover package: deep clean, AC service, minor paint touch-ups, lock change, and condition report — all in one scheduled visit. From AED 800 for a 1-bed turnover package.

Some JVC streets still lack clear addresses

JVC's circular layout and frequent new construction means GPS often sends drivers to the wrong building. We ask for the building name on booking and the JVC district number — pin location via WhatsApp recommended for new owners. Drivers know the area well after thousands of jobs.
