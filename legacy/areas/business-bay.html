What's Different About Service in Business Bay

Mixed-use building access is more complex

Business Bay towers blend residential, office and retail floors under one building management. That means three different sets of access rules per building, and different timing restrictions depending on floor. Our team is trained on the specific protocols of each major tower — for example, Executive Towers Tower G has separate residential and commercial security desks.

After-hours office work is the norm, not the exception

Most Business Bay offices can't afford weekday downtime for cleaning, painting or AC work. We default to evening (6 PM onwards) and weekend slots for office work, with building-management notice handled by us.

F&B and retail need Civil Defence-approved contractors

Bay Avenue and Bay Square ground floors are dense with F&B and retail. Any fit-out, electrical or fire-system work in these units requires Civil Defence approval. We're cleared as approved contractors for residential and commercial work — full paperwork handled.

Short-let and holiday rental support

A high percentage of Business Bay apartments operate as short-lets through Airbnb, Frank Porter, Bnbme and direct rental. Same-day turnover (clean, linen change, check report) from AED 250 per apartment.
