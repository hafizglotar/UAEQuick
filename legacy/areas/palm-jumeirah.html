Why Palm Jumeirah Villas Need Specialist Service

Salt-air destroys outdoor equipment 3-4x faster

Palm Jumeirah is surrounded by sea on three sides. Outdoor AC condenser units corrode at 3 to 4 times the rate of mainland Dubai installations. Untreated, a new AC condenser can lose 30% efficiency within 18 months. Our Palm AC service includes ultrasonic descaling, marine-grade aluminium coating and replacement of failed condenser fins. Most villas we maintain see condenser life extended to 8-10 years.

Salt-water pools are common - and tricky

Many Palm villas have salt-water pools (with chlorinator cells instead of liquid chlorine). These need different maintenance: salt level testing, chlorinator cell cleaning every 3 months, and stainless-steel handrail descaling. Our pool team is salt-water trained - not all Dubai pool companies are.

Palm tree health is non-negotiable

The signature palms on every Frond villa are vulnerable to red palm weevil - an aggressive pest that can kill a mature date palm in 6 months. Weekly inspection, preventive treatment and early-stage injection are part of our standard Palm garden contract. Replacing a mature palm costs AED 8,000-15,000 - preventive care is AED 300/month.

Painting requires marine-grade specification

Standard Dubai exterior paint fails within 2-3 years on Palm Jumeirah. We specify Jotun Hardtop AS or equivalent marine-grade paint for exteriors, with proper primer and 3-coat application. Job takes 7-14 days for a full villa exterior, but lasts 6-8 years versus 2-3 for standard paint.
