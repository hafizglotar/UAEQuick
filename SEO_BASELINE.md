# SEO baseline - uaequick.com

Snapshot of every indexable route, taken from a production build of branch `main` at commit `9d24f29` on 2026-11-21, before any semantic SEO work.

**This is the do-not-touch reference.** URL, title, meta description, H1, canonical, robots and Open Graph tags of every route below must be identical after the work. `seo-baseline.json` holds the same data in machine-readable form, including Open Graph tags and the full link lists, and is what the final check diffs against.

## How the site is built

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind. No `pages/` router.
- **Hosting:** Vercel. Canonical host `https://www.uaequick.com`; Vercel redirects the bare domain. No trailing slashes. No host redirects in code.
- **Content:** typed data files, not a CMS: `lib/data/services.ts`, `lib/data/areas.ts`, `lib/data/blog.ts`, `lib/data/site.ts`.
- **Metadata:** every route calls `buildMetadata()` in `lib/seo/metadata.ts`, which sets title, description, self canonical, robots `index, follow`, Open Graph and Twitter tags.
- **Schema:** JSON-LD builders in `lib/seo/schema.ts`, rendered by `components/JsonLd.tsx`. Organization, WebSite and HomeAndConstructionBusiness are emitted sitewide from `app/layout.tsx`.
- **Sitemap:** `app/sitemap.ts`, generated from the data files, with real content dates from `lib/data/updated.ts`.
- **Robots:** `app/robots.ts`. Allows everything except `/api/`, with AI crawlers listed explicitly.
- **Redirects:** `next.config.mjs`, legacy WordPress paths only.
- **hreflang:** none. Single language, `<html lang="en-AE">`.

## Phase 0 findings

### What is already in place

- No duplicate titles, descriptions, H1s or canonicals across 176 routes.
- No route is missing schema, and every JSON-LD block parses as valid JSON.
- `BreadcrumbList` is on every route except the home page, where it is not needed.
- `FAQPage` is on every route with a visible FAQ section (home, services index, 9 services, 14 areas, 126 service x area pages, 18 posts).
- `Service` with a from-price `Offer` is on all 9 service pages and all 126 service x area pages.
- `Article` is on all 18 posts.

### Missing schema

- **`aggregateRating`:** absent sitewide. The "4.8 from 412+" figure is shown on the page but has not been confirmed against the Google Business Profile, so it should stay out of schema until it is.
- **Blog author:** `Article.author` is an `Organization` ("UAE Quick Team"). There is no `Person` author.
- **Home page:** no `WebPage` node of its own.
- **`/quote`, `/privacy`, `/terms`:** breadcrumbs only, no `WebPage` node.

### Orphan and weakly linked pages

- **`/about` is an orphan.** It is in the sitemap, but no page links to it, not even the header or footer.
- **`/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`:** reachable from the header or footer only, with no in-content links. Acceptable for utility pages.
- **15 of 18 blog posts have a single inbound link**, from the `/blog` index. Service pages, area pages and other posts do not link to them.
- **Blog posts link out weakly.** 16 of 18 link to services only through the "Related service" chip, not from the body text.
- **Thinnest service x area pages for inbound links:** `/services/movers-packers/emirates-hills` (2), `/services/movers-packers/mirdif`, `/services/movers-packers/silicon-oasis`, `/services/interior-design/emirates-hills` (3 each).
- **Area pages** average 353 words in the body, the thinnest content group that is expected to rank.

### Titles and descriptions (reported only, not to be changed)

- 37 titles are longer than 60 characters, mostly area pages at 66 to 72. Google may truncate them.
- One description is over 160 characters: `/blog/move-out-cleaning-checklist-ejari-handover-dubai` at 163.

### Tooling

- `npm run lint` does not work. `next lint` was removed in Next.js 16 and the repo has no ESLint config. The final check can run `tsc --noEmit` and the build; a real lint step needs ESLint set up first.

## Conflicts between the brief and the existing site

These need a decision before the phases they affect.

1. **Phase 3, same-day hub pages, would cannibalize the service pages.** All 9 service pages already target the same-day keyword: the title of `/services/ac-repair` is "Same Day AC Repair in Dubai | From AED 150 | UAE Quick" and its H1 is "Same-Day AC Repair in Dubai". A new `/same-day/ac-repair` page would compete for the exact same main keyword, which the brief's own rules forbid. The same holds for cleaning, electrician and handyman-plumber.
2. **Phase 5, service x area pages, already exist.** There are 126 of them at `/services/{service}/{area}`, for example `/services/ac-repair/jvc` titled "Same Day AC Repair in JVC | UAE Quick". Adding `/areas/jvc/ac-repair` would create 15 direct duplicates of existing pages.
3. **Phase 2, schema, is almost entirely done.** Organization, HomeAndConstructionBusiness, Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList and Article are all live. The only additive work left is the list under "Missing schema".
4. **Handyman and plumber are one service.** The brief lists them separately for the same-day hub, but the site has a single `/services/handyman-plumber` page.
5. **There are 9 public services, and movers and packers is one of them.** `vehicle-recovery` exists in the data but has no public page.
