# Topical map - uaequick.com

Plan for semantic coverage of the 9 services. Every proposed URL was checked against `seo-baseline.json`: none exists today, and none targets the main keyword of an existing page.

## Rules this map follows

- **Service pages own "same day {service} Dubai".** All 9 already carry that keyword in title and H1, so no per-service same-day pages are created. One `/same-day` hub explains how same-day booking works and links to them.
- **Service x area pages already exist** at `/services/{service}/{area}` (126 pages). No new area pages are proposed.
- **Sub-service pages own one specific job each**, for example "AC gas refill Dubai". They live at `/services/{service}/{sub-service}`, link up to the parent, and only quote prices already published on the parent page.
- **Blog posts own questions**: cost, how often, why, DIY vs professional.
- A sub-service is marked "later" when the site has no published price for it or an existing post already targets its keyword.

Status key: **Build now** is built in this round. **Later** is mapped but not built. **Exists** is already live.
